CloudKeep

Cloud storage that works: upload, download, share links and sign-in through Cognito. Files go straight to the bucket with presigned URLs, so they never pass through my server. It isn’t finished — quotas, lifecycle rules and a few edges are still open.

Discord Bridge

Omegle, but for Discord channels. Two servers link a channel and the conversation is shared both ways — messages cross as webhooks so they look like they were sent there, with edits, deletes and sticky notices kept in sync. Random mode pairs your channel with a stranger’s.
